Method for trees

Brush, palmetto and saplings are covered by the density rate. Count trees 6" and up. Only count under-6" trees when they’re scattered in otherwise open ground.

Pines small ones pulled, big ones felled + stumps dug

Under-8" pines come out with the tree puller, tree and stump pulled up together, so there is no separate stump work. Pines 8" and up are felled first, then the stump is dug out. Count pines here, not in Trees above.

What happens to the debris

Burn days used
Burn days are machine days on site to feed, push in and restack piles. Type your own number to override, or adjust the inputs below.

Default pile is about 150 cu yd (roughly 30 ft across × 12 ft high).

Burn authorization: piles need a burn authorization from the Florida Forest Service before you light. Setbacks (homes, roads, woods), burn hours, pile size, and county or city rules can change, and burn bans happen. Check current FFS and county rules for this site before you light.

Loads = piled cu yd × swell ÷ trailer size. Haul time counts as your time (one-man: the machine sits while you drive). Dump fees are a pass-through cost with your markup. Check the facility’s current yard-waste rate.

Windrows are pushed into place during clearing, so no extra days are added.
